What's new

What's new

The launcher, the game and the phone app get every new version automatically.

  1. 1.1.32

    November 19, 2026
    • Tier Tagger: bigger look hitbox – the tier card now also shows when your crosshair is just next to a player, a bit more generous at a distance. Adjustable under "Look Hitbox".
    • Android app: the welcome steps got stuck at the design – fixed. Mod bundles now install on phones too, the chosen design also applies in game, and on the meadow the petals fly from the flowers again.

  2. 1.1.31

    November 19, 2026
    • Tiers on look: look at a player who is on the tier list and their tier card shows above the hotbar – also at a distance (up to 24 blocks, adjustable) and never through walls. C still shows your last opponent.

  3. 1.1.30

    November 18, 2026
    • Tier card: press C (configurable in Tier Tagger) to see the MCTiers rankings of the last player you hit or who hit you – face, name and peak tier, centred above the hotbar.
    • Profiles open instantly: the launcher no longer re-reads folder sizes, worlds and screenshots every time; it remembers them and refreshes in the background.
    • One in-game menu: Right Shift, Modules and Client settings all open the same clean Onix menu.
    • The Mac download is back.

  4. 1.1.29

    November 18, 2026
    • Runner: the character now faces the way it runs (it ran backwards), takes longer strides, leans into the run and runs in every time the main menu opens.
    • Meadow at night: a black sky with a big red moon; the flowers and petals are lit by it.
    • The petals fly all the way up and out of the picture instead of fading away.

  5. 1.1.28

    November 17, 2026
    • A new look: the launcher, the menus in game and the website now share one calm, dark design with a blue accent and the Poppins typeface. The launcher has its pages in a bar along the top.
    • Three designs for the start page, the start screen and the main menu in game: Water (you sink through deep water), Runner (your character runs into a dark studio) and Meadow (thousands of flowers – when you start, a gust runs through the meadow and every flower lets go of its petals).
    • Launcher in German and English (Settings → Language).
    • Themes, backgrounds and custom skies are gone – the designs replace them.

  6. 1.1.27

    November 16, 2026
    • Take a profile to another version: Profile → Other Version copies your whole modpack – worlds, Onix modules and HUD, key binds, settings, servers, mod configs and packs – to another Minecraft version, with every mod swapped for its version there. Your original profile stays as it is.

  7. 1.1.26

    November 16, 2026
    • Tier Tagger: the PvP tier of every player from MCTiers (or SubTiers) next to their name – in nametags and the tab list, for example HT3 in Sword, or the best tier over all modes. Turn it on in the module menu (Combat).

  8. 1.1.25

    November 15, 2026
    • Mod bundles: hand-picked mod sets – Quality of Life, Visuals, Sound, Extra FPS and Content Creator – go into a profile with one click (Profile → Bundles).
    • Welcome steps: on the first start Onix helps you sign in, pick a theme and choose bundles.
    • Livelier water animation: the falling character floats loosely, arms and legs trail behind with the motion, and the water bends the light.

  9. 1.1.24

    November 12, 2026
    • Updates when your PC starts: Onix updates itself quietly in the background when you start your computer and closes again – no window opens. You can turn it off in the launcher settings.
    • Water on the graphics card: caustics, light shafts that flicker with the waves, a rippling surface and drifting specks – in the launcher and in game.
    • The falling character's legs are a little straighter.

  10. 1.1.23

    November 12, 2026
    • Water animation, made new: the surface shimmers above, light rays fall into the water, air bubbles rise. Your character falls backwards with arms and feet up, lit from above.

  11. 1.1.22

    November 12, 2026
    • Shaders only when you want them: they no longer turn on by themselves. Turn them on in the launcher under Profile → Shaders.
    • Essential in the main menu: with Essential installed, three buttons at the top right open its friends, wardrobe and pictures.
    • More FPS: BadOptimizations and ScalableLux join the launcher's performance pack.
    • The HUD editor's buttons now always sit above the HUD elements.

  12. 1.1.21

    November 12, 2026
    • Minimap and world map: terrain from above with height shading, players, mobs and waypoints. M opens the big map to drag and zoom.
    • Waypoint beams: every waypoint shines as a beam of light in the world.
    • Motion blur in three strengths.
    • Item physics: dropped items lie flat on the ground.
    • Hit colour: pick the colour enemies flash when you hit them.
    • Chat copy: Ctrl + click a message to copy it.
    • Rejoin: a button in the main menu takes you back to your last server.
    • A new start screen in the launcher and a new main menu in game, both under water.
