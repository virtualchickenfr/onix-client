What's new

The launcher, the game and the phone app get every new version automatically.

  1. 1.1.24

    November 12, 2026
    • Updates when your PC starts: Onix updates itself quietly in the background when you start your computer and closes again – no window opens. You can turn it off in the launcher settings.
    • Water on the graphics card: caustics, light shafts that flicker with the waves, a rippling surface and drifting specks – in the launcher and in game.
    • The falling character's legs are a little straighter.

  2. 1.1.23

    November 12, 2026
    • Water animation, made new: the surface shimmers above, light rays fall into the water, air bubbles rise. Your character falls backwards with arms and feet up, lit from above.

  3. 1.1.22

    November 12, 2026
    • Shaders only when you want them: they no longer turn on by themselves. Turn them on in the launcher under Profile → Shaders.
    • Essential in the main menu: with Essential installed, three buttons at the top right open its friends, wardrobe and pictures.
    • More FPS: BadOptimizations and ScalableLux join the launcher's performance pack.
    • The HUD editor's buttons now always sit above the HUD elements.

  4. 1.1.21

    November 12, 2026
    • Minimap and world map: terrain from above with height shading, players, mobs and waypoints. M opens the big map to drag and zoom.
    • Waypoint beams: every waypoint shines as a beam of light in the world.
    • Motion blur in three strengths.
    • Item physics: dropped items lie flat on the ground.
    • Hit colour: pick the colour enemies flash when you hit them.
    • Chat copy: Ctrl + click a message to copy it.
    • Rejoin: a button in the main menu takes you back to your last server.
    • A new start screen in the launcher and a new main menu in game, both under water.
