Download Onix

The launcher installs Minecraft, Fabric, Onix and the performance mods and keeps everything up to date.

 

WindowsInstaller (.exe) macOSApple chip & Intel (.dmg) LinuxAppImage AndroidApp (.apk)

Install

Windows

  1. Download Onix-Client-Setup.exe and run it.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info and then Run anyway. The installer is not signed by Microsoft.
  3. The launcher opens. Sign in with your Microsoft account and click Play.

macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Onix Client into Applications.
  2. On the first start macOS says the app cannot be checked. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to Onix Client.
  3. Sign in and click Play.

Linux

  1. Download the AppImage.
  2. Make it executable: right click → Properties → “Allow executing file as program”, or in a terminal chmod +x Onix-Client-*.AppImage.
  3. Start it, sign in and click Play.

Android

  1. Download Onix-Android.apk on your phone and open it.
  2. Allow your browser to install apps once (Android asks you on its own).
  3. Open Onix, sign in and tap Play. The app installs new versions by itself.

What you need

  • Minecraft Java EditionYour own, bought account. You sign in with Microsoft.
  • 4 GB of memory8 GB recommended. You choose how much Minecraft gets per profile in the launcher.
  • Internet on the first startThe launcher downloads Minecraft and the mods once. It brings its own Java.
  • Android 8 or newerFor the phone app. A recent device with 6 GB of memory runs best.

Just the mod

Already using your own launcher with Fabric? Take the .jar for your Minecraft version and put it in the mods folder. Fabric API is needed.
