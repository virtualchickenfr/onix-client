Performance 21

More FPS and fewer stutters.

  • Background MuteMute or lower sound when you tab out.
  • Cloud RangeHow far away clouds are drawn.
  • Entity DistanceRender entities closer or further away.
  • Low FPS WarningWarns when your frame rate drops below 30.
  • Memory WarningWarns when Minecraft is close to running out of memory.
  • Minimal ParticlesSets particles to minimal.
  • No Biome BlendDisables color blending between biomes (faster chunk building).
  • No CloudsHides the clouds.
  • No Entity ShadowsRemoves the round shadows under mobs and players.
  • No Menu BlurRemoves the background blur behind menus.
  • No MipmapsDisables mipmaps for slightly more FPS.
  • No Smooth LightingTurns off ambient occlusion for more FPS.
  • Particle LimiterReduces the number of particles for more FPS (from Carbon).
  • Performance PresetOne switch for FPS-friendly video settings.
  • Render Distance LockHolds the render distance at a fixed value.
  • Render Distance SwitchToggle between two render distances.
  • ShadersTurns the shader pack on and off (needs Iris; the launcher's one-click shaders add it).
  • Simulation DistanceHolds the simulation distance at a fixed value.
  • Unfocused FPSSaves power by limiting FPS when you tab out.
  • Unlimited FPSRemoves the frame rate limit.
  • VSync OffTurns off vertical sync for lower input delay.
