# Privacy

Onix Client (the launcher and the mod) contains **no analytics, tracking or advertising** and does not send
data anywhere except to the services listed here, and only for the purpose named.

## Launcher
| Service | What for | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft, Xbox Live, Minecraft services | Signing in to your Minecraft account | Your Microsoft sign-in (handled by Microsoft's own sign-in page) |
| Mojang (piston-meta.mojang.com and download servers) | Downloading Minecraft 1.21.1 | Nothing personal |
| Fabric (meta.fabricmc.net and maven) | Downloading the Fabric loader | Nothing personal |
| Modrinth (api.modrinth.com, cdn.modrinth.com) | Fabric API and Sodium updates, the mod and modpack browser | Search words you type, file hashes of your mods when you check for updates |
| Adoptium (api.adoptium.net) | Downloading Java 21 if you have none | Your operating system and processor type |
| GitHub (github.com) | Checking for and downloading launcher updates – can be turned off in *Settings → Launcher updates* | Your launcher version |
| mc-heads.net | Showing player heads in the launcher | Minecraft names/UUIDs of your accounts |
| Your Onix server (only if you enter one) | Badges, coins, shop and gifts | Your Minecraft name and UUID, play time, purchases and gifts |

## Mod
| Service | What for | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Your Onix server (only if one is set) | Onix badges, coins, shop and gifts | Your Minecraft name and UUID, whether you are playing, the UUIDs of players near you (to show their badge), purchases and gifts |
| Mojang session server | Proving to your Onix server that you own your account (the same check Minecraft servers use) | A one-time server id |

Account tokens are stored on your computer only, encrypted with your operating system's key store.
Everything else (profiles, settings, cosmetics) is stored in your Minecraft folder. Nothing is shared with the
developers of Onix Client.
