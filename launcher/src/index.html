Import

Bring everything from other clients and launchers into an Onix profile.

Settings, key binds, servers, worlds, resource and shader packs, screenshots and compatible Fabric mods are copied completely. Which client modules (Keystrokes, Zoom, …) were enabled in Lunar, Feather, Badlion or LabyMod is detected from their files. This part is in beta. Cosmetics from other clients belong to your account there and cannot be transferred.

Install

Bring Onix and your profiles into games and launchers outside the Onix Launcher.

Running now

A Fabric mod has to be there when Minecraft starts, so Onix is never injected into a running game. If a game already has Onix, Send Profile loads your profile into it instantly.

Other launchers
Download the mod
Onix ClientFor Fabric · Minecraft 1.21.1

Put the files into the mods folder of any Fabric 1.21.1 installation (Fabric Loader 0.15 or newer). Press Right Shift in game to open Onix.

Settings

Applies to every profile. Memory and servers are set per profile.

Theme

Themes are shared with the game: a theme you make here shows up in the Onix menu and the other way round.

Background

Used here and on the in-game title screen. Your own images: put PNG files into config/onix/backgrounds of a profile.

In-game sky
Custom skyReplaces the overworld sky. Day and night skies fade into each other with the time of day.

Applies to every profile. If Minecraft is running with Onix, the new sky shows up right away. More options (brightness, rotation, fog) are in the Custom Sky module in game.
